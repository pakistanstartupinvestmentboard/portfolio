03 · The methodHow I work

Four channels. For each one: what most people do, and what works instead.

01 / 04 · Outbound

What most people doPersonalize the first line, template the rest, then wait for a reply.

Personalization isn’t a first line.

Find one real problem on their website or socials and build the whole message around it: what you saw, the pain it causes, one small ask. Every sentence is about them.

02 / 04 · Inbound

What most people doPost content, then wait for the right people to find it.

Inbound isn’t waiting.

Source the exact people worth hearing from, then show up in their comments with something genuinely useful. Over a few weeks they see the expertise, and the first question comes from them.

03 / 04 · Meta ads

What most people doLaunch one ad, then guess why it did or didn’t work.

AI makes the variants. Data picks the winner.

One offer becomes many ads: different angles (benefit, purpose, pain) in different formats. AI produces them fast, and the budget follows whatever converts, not whatever looks best.

04 / 04 · Lead scoring

What most people doScore leads on industry, company size and job title.

Score the pain, not the profile.

Two companies can look identical on paper. The signals that matter sit in their hiring, their posts, their website and their workflows. Tap to find them.
